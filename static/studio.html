        What this is — and what it is not.

        It is a deterministic agent-based rehearsal. Synthetic citizens are drawn from public distributions of state, tier, income, digital access and occupation. Each decision is an explicit function of affordability, relative advantage, social proof, trust and perceived risk.

        It is not a representative survey, a poll, or a prediction of any individual. The confidence score is a model-stability indicator, not a sampling error. Treat every output as a hypothesis to test with real people.

        Forecasts use Google Research's TimesFM 3.0 in zero-shot mode on cited monthly series. TimesFM weights are non-commercial. A fallback baseline is labelled explicitly if the model is unavailable.
